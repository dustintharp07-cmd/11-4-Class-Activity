Data and methodology

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024 5-Year Estimates, Detailed Table B02001 — Race. The estimates are downloaded from the Census Bureau’s public 2024 table-based ACS Summary File, then joined to official 2024 TIGER/Line county boundaries by five-digit county GEOID. All 67 Florida counties are included.

Each mapped value is the selected race-alone estimate divided by the county’s B02001 total population estimate, multiplied by 100. ACS figures are survey estimates. Census race-alone categories describe race; Hispanic or Latino origin is a separate ethnicity classification and is not included as a mutually exclusive race category in this map.
